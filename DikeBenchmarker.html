Benchmarking Job that behaves like a future.

Identity: benchmark_id, solver_id, created_at (ctor time).

Lifecycle:

CREATED (initial)

–[put into JobLog]–> SUBMITTED –[start working on]–> RUNNING –[finish working on]–> FINISHED | FAILED
